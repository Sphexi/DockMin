What the archive holds

  • Every file of the volume under data/, with its mode, numeric owner ids, timestamps and symbolic links.
  • manifest.json: the volume's name, driver, labels and driver options. Credentials in driver options are left out and listed by key.
  • Device files and FIFOs are left out and listed in skipped.json. Sockets are not read.

The archive is not encrypted

The archive holds the volume's files as they are. Anyone who holds the file can read everything in the volume.
